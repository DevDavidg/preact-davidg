  earth.webp    from "2k_earth_daymap"        — 2048x1024, 129 kB
  night.webp    from "2k_earth_nightmap"      — 2048x1024,  91 kB
  moon.webp     from "2k_moon"                — 1536x768,  135 kB
  saturn.webp   from "2k_saturn"              — 2048x1024,  48 kB
  ring.webp     from "2k_saturn_ring_alpha"   — 1024x8,      2 kB

  Changes: converted to WebP; quality chosen per map to stay under the site's
  150 kB per-image budget (see scripts/bundle-budget.mjs). The moon was reduced to
  1536x768 because it is high-frequency across its whole surface and 2K lossy could
  not reach the budget without visible blocking in the crater rims. The ring alpha
  strip was reduced to eight rows: it varies with radius only. Rendered with custom
  lighting, not with the source maps' own shading.

three.js authors — MIT
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets

  earth-mask.webp  from "earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096" — 2048x1024, 143 kB

  A channel-packed map: R is elevation, G is roughness (land is rough, water is
  glass) and B is cloud cover. Changes: resized to 2048x1024 and converted to WebP.
  Used as the height field for the terminator's relief, the mask that keeps the
  specular on the ocean, and the cloud deck — three maps in one fetch.
